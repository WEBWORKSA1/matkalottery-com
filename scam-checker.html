    How the checker decides

    It looks for 15 patterns that appear again and again in Indian lottery and prize frauds: fees before a prize, OTP or PIN requests, famous brand names, payment apps and QR codes, foreign numbers, urgency, links, guaranteed wins, foreign lotteries, ID requests, secrecy, refund tricks, online Kerala ticket offers and matka “fix numbers”. Each adds to a risk score.

    It can miss new tricks and can’t prove a message is genuine. When in doubt, verify through the organisation’s official website — never through details in the message.
