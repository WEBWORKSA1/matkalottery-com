Random number generator

Fair, unpredictable picks for raffles, classroom draws and games, made with your browser’s cryptographic random source.

0 = no bonus numbers

An honest note. Random picks have exactly the same chance as any numbers you choose yourself — no generator, chart or system can improve lottery odds. Anyone selling “lucky” or “fix” numbers is selling a lie.

Good uses

  • Picking raffle winners at a school or society event
  • Assigning turns, teams or seats fairly
  • Teaching probability in class

Numbers are made with crypto.getRandomValues, the same source browsers use for security, with rejection sampling so every number is equally likely.
