What happens next

  1. We read your request and match it to one professional: a CA for tax and claims, a planner for investing a windfall, or a lawyer for fraud and disputes.
  2. They call or WhatsApp you, usually within one working day, for a free first conversation.
  3. If you want more help, they quote their fee to you directly. You decide.

Meanwhile, protect yourself

  • Sign the back of a winning ticket and keep it somewhere safe. Don’t post it online.
  • If you lost money, call 1930 now — speed matters more than paperwork.
  • Ignore anyone else who contacts you claiming to be from us or from a lottery office.

Questions

Is the review really free?

Yes. We don’t charge you. Professionals may pay us an introduction fee, which never adds to what they charge you.

Who are the professionals?

Practising chartered accountants, SEBI-registered or fee-only financial planners and advocates we have checked for valid registration and good standing.

Can you get my scammed money back?

No one can promise that, and anyone who does is likely running a recovery scam. We help you file quickly through official routes and connect you with a lawyer if your case needs one.
