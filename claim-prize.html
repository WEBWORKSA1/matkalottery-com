How to claim a lottery prize safely

By the MatkaLottery.com editorial team. Last checked 12 November 2026. 6 minute read.

The rule that beats every fake agent: you go to the lottery department; it never comes to you. No genuine office calls winners first, and nobody can speed up a claim for a fee.

The first hour

  1. Check the result on the state’s official lottery website or Government Gazette — the full series and number, not a forwarded screenshot.
  2. Sign your name and write your address on the back of the ticket.
  3. Photograph both sides and keep the images private. Never post a winning ticket online: fraudsters use those details to file fake claims or to target you.
  4. Store the original flat and dry, somewhere only you can reach.
  5. Tell as few people as possible until the money is in your account.

Claiming a Kerala prize

Small prizes are typically paid out by authorised lottery agents. Larger prizes are claimed through the Directorate of State Lotteries, the district lottery offices or a bank, and Kerala requires winners to surrender the ticket within 30 days of the draw. Expect to submit:

  • the original signed ticket and the prescribed claim form,
  • recent passport-size photographs,
  • identity and address proof, and your PAN (needed for TDS),
  • bank account details for the transfer, and a stamped receipt if the form asks for one.

The exact form, thresholds and document list are set by the department and change from time to time — download the current version from the official website.

Other state lotteries

Every state notifies its own claim procedure and deadline. Look for the claim form and instructions on the state’s official lottery website, submit in person or by the method it names, and keep a stamped acknowledgement of everything you hand over.

Prizes from companies, apps and contests

Brands do run legitimate contests, but the scam versions copy them closely. Before you share documents or pay anything:

  • Find the company’s official website yourself and check that the contest exists and that you entered it.
  • Contact the company only through numbers or emails listed on that website.
  • For a prize in kind, a genuine company may collect TDS from you — but it will issue proper paperwork and never ask you to pay into a personal account or UPI ID.

The first month: protect the money

  • Keep the prize in a bank deposit while you plan. There is no deadline on investing it.
  • Work out and pay the remaining tax — surcharge and cess are usually still due. See lottery tax in India.
  • Speak to a chartered accountant and a fee-only financial planner before you commit to anything.
  • Say no to every investment pitch, loan request and “business opportunity” for at least 90 days.
  • Update nominees and write a will.

Kerala’s lottery department has even run financial-planning sessions for big winners, because so many windfalls disappear quickly.
