Is gambling costing you more than money?

Nine private questions about lottery tickets, betting, online games or matka over the last 12 months. Your answers stay on your device.

1. How often have you spent more on tickets, bets or games than you planned?
2. How often have you needed to stake more to get the same excitement?
3. How often have you gone back another day to win back what you lost?
4. How often have you borrowed money or sold something to keep playing?
5. How often has playing caused money problems for you or your household?
6. How often have people close to you said your playing is a problem?
7. How often have you felt guilty about how much you play?
8. How often have you hidden tickets, slips or spending from family?
9. How often has playing left you stressed, anxious or unable to sleep?

This reflection tool draws on common warning signs. It is not a diagnosis; a counsellor can give you a proper assessment.

Free, confidential help

Call 14416 or 1-800-891-4416

+91 91529 87821

Online, anywhere

Gambling Therapy offers free online support worldwide, and Gamblers Anonymous runs peer meetings, including online.

Small steps that help today

  • Delete betting and “result” apps, and leave the groups that send numbers.
  • Let someone you trust hold your cards or UPI for a few weeks.
  • Write down what you’ve spent this month. Seeing it is often the turning point.
  • Never borrow to play, and never play to repay.

Worried about someone else? Talk without blame, offer to make the first call together, and protect household money while they get help.
