Official rules (short version)

  1. Free to enter. No purchase, ticket or payment of any kind is needed or accepted.
  2. Open to residents of India aged 18 or over. One entry per person per contest.
  3. Entries must be your own original work and must not promote betting, matka or any gambling.
  4. A panel judges every entry against the published criteria. Winners are chosen on merit only.
  5. Winners are announced on this page within 15 days of the closing date and paid by bank transfer or UPI within 30 days, after a simple identity check.
  6. You keep the rights to your work and give MatkaLottery.com permission to publish it with credit.

We will never ask a winner to pay a fee, tax or “processing charge” to receive a prize. If anyone does, it is a scam — report it to 1930.
