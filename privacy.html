Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026.

This policy explains what information MatkaLottery.com (“we”) collects, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can.

Information you give us

When you send a form — contact, help request, newsletter, pledge, contest entry or job application — we receive what you type, such as your name, email, phone number and message. Forms are delivered to our team inbox through FormSubmit, a form-delivery service. If your browser can’t reach that service, your email app opens instead so you can send the message yourself.

When you ask for a winner or scam-victim review and tick the consent box, we share your request with one vetted professional (a chartered accountant, financial planner or advocate) so they can contact you. We do not sell your information.

Information that stays on your device

Everything you enter in our tools — prize amounts, pasted messages, quiz answers — is processed in your browser and is not sent to us. We use your browser’s local storage only to remember your cookie choice.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Other services we use

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts delivers our typefaces.
  • YouTube videos load in privacy-enhanced mode, and only after you press play.
  • Google News and rss2json supply the headlines on our news sections; your browser requests them directly.
  • FormSubmit delivers form messages to us.

How long we keep information

We keep messages and requests only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, normally no more than 24 months. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data you sent us, or withdraw your consent. Use our contact form and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 18. Contests are open only to adults.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Never send ticket numbers, OTPs, PINs or bank passwords through any form — we will never ask for them.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our contact form.
