Help millions spot the scam before they pay

We’re a small, remote team building India’s most trusted plain-language guide to lotteries, prize tax and fraud. All roles are flexible and paid per piece, per hour or on commission.

Tax & personal-finance writer

Explain TDS, surcharge and windfall planning in plain words. CA, CS or CMA students and practising professionals welcome.

Freelance, paid per article.

Legal explainer writer

Turn gambling, lottery and cyber-fraud law into clear guides. Law students and advocates welcome.

Freelance, paid per article.

Regional-language translators

Hindi, Marathi, Malayalam, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu. Accuracy and a natural, everyday tone matter most.

Freelance, paid per word.

Short-video creator

Turn our guides into 60-second reels and shorts that people actually forward to their parents.

Freelance, paid per video.

Fact-checker & researcher

Verify every figure against laws, official notifications and reliable reporting before we publish.

Part-time, paid hourly.

Partnerships & ad sales

Bring in banks, fintechs, CA firms and CSR teams for sponsorships and awareness campaigns.

Commission-based.

We never charge applicants a registration, training or “security deposit” fee. Job offers that ask you to pay are scams.

How hiring works

  1. Send the form with a link to two or three samples of your work.
  2. If there’s a fit, we send a short paid trial task.
  3. Good trials become regular work, with clear rates agreed upfront.

We’re also happy to hear from volunteers who want to help with translation or community moderation.
