About MatkaLottery.com

Millions of people in India search for “matka” and “lottery” every month. Most of what they find sells false hope or steals money. We built the honest alternative.

What we do

  • Explain how lotteries, odds and prize taxes actually work, in plain language.
  • Document the history of matka and what Indian law says about gambling today.
  • Build free tools that answer real questions: “How much will I keep?”, “Is this message a scam?”
  • Connect prize winners and scam victims with vetted professionals.

What we never do

  • Publish lottery or matka results, charts, “guessing” or “fix” numbers.
  • Sell tickets, run games, or link to betting, matka or online money-game operators.
  • Accept advertising from gambling businesses.

Editorial policy

Sources

We rely first on primary sources — Acts of Parliament and state legislatures, official notifications, tax department guidance and official lottery publications — then on reputable legal, financial and news reporting. Every guide lists its main sources.

Accuracy and review

Each guide shows the date it was last checked. We review legal and tax pages whenever the law changes and at least every six months. Figures in tools are explained on the same page.

Corrections

If something is wrong, tell us. We correct factual errors promptly and note significant corrections on the page.

Independence

We are not affiliated with any lottery department, operator, bank or government body. Sponsored content and partner tools are always labelled, and sponsors have no say over our guides.

Not professional advice

Our guides are general information. For decisions about tax, investments or legal action, speak to a qualified professional — our free review can connect you with one.

How we’re funded

Display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, introduction fees from vetted professionals, and contributions from readers. Read more on supporting us and partnering with us.

Contact

Use our contact form. For enquiries about this website, the domain name, sponsorship, advertising or partnership, contact the owner at web.works/contact.
